---
title: "Teaching philosophy"
---

People learn entrepreneurship by practising it, not by memorising it. Since 2019 I have taught six courses at Boston International College, using workshops, real cases, projects, experiential learning and role-play simulations. In one simulation, students build and pitch a minimum viable product (MVP); my 2026 article studied how MBA graduates experienced it.

Three principles guide my teaching:

- **Learn by doing.** Entrepreneurship is a practice. People learn it best by testing ideas, not just reading about them.
- **Keep it simple.** Good tools are simple enough to use the next morning.
- **Connect the classroom to the real world.** Students, industry and government grow faster together.
