---
title: "Research agenda"
---

My research asks how entrepreneurship can be taught well and how startup ecosystems grow in Nepal. It sits where three things meet: the classroom, the incubator, and the regional economy.

**Entrepreneurship education.** How experiential methods, such as startup simulations built around a minimum viable product, shape what students learn, and how teachers integrate digital technologies into business-school classrooms.

**Startup innovation and incubation.** How business incubators evolve, how they are distributed, and what it takes for university-based incubation to support new ventures.

**Regional startup ecosystems.** How industry, academia and government interact in a region such as Chitwan, and what opportunities and constraints local entrepreneurs face.

I plan to deepen this agenda through doctoral research, and I welcome collaboration with researchers working on entrepreneurship education and startup ecosystems in Nepal and comparable economies.
