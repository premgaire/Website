---
title: "Entrepreneurship and innovation"
---

Alongside teaching and research, I work on the practical side of Nepal's entrepreneurship ecosystem: helping students turn ideas into ventures, and helping institutions build programs that support them.

For five years I was Assistant Director of BIC Startups, the business incubation program of Boston International College, where I oversaw planning, training, seed-fund disbursement and program documentation. As focal person for the college's Industry Connect program, I built links between students, local industry and government. I also trained as an incubator manager through Skill Lab's *Incubating the Incubators* Training of Trainers program and took part in the Global Entrepreneurship Bootcamp in Bangkok.
