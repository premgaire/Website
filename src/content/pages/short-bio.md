title: "Short biography (third person)"
---

Prem Nath Gaire is a lecturer and early-career researcher based in Chitwan, Nepal. He teaches entrepreneurship and management at Boston International College, where he led the BIC Startups incubation program from 2019 to 2024. He holds a Master of Management in Entrepreneurship and Innovation from La Trobe University, Australia. He has secured USD 40,000 in national entrepreneurship funding and a UGC Nepal Faculty Research Grant, and delivers workshops for colleges, NGOs and incubators. His research covers startup innovation, entrepreneurship education and digital technologies.
